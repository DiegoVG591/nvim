# Credits

Base options, and several keymaps (telescope's grep pattern,
fugitive's push/pull-rebase binds, the LSP on_attach structure,
undotree, ColorMyPencils) are adapted from ThePrimeagen's "0 to LSP"
neovim series and dotfiles (github.com/ThePrimeagen/init.lua).

Uses ThePrimeagen's own plugins as dependencies: harpoon, vim-be-good.

Language tooling choices, the run-src integration, and other
customizations are original.
